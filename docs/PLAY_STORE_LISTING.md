## Full description — English
Flosi helps you understand and organize your money in one clear place. Track accounts, income and expenses, plan budgets, build savings goals, follow commitments and bills, manage personal debts, create invoices and review useful analytics.

Designed for Arabic-first use with RTL support and a clean modern interface, Flosi also supports multiple currencies, secure backups, optional biometric protection, light/dark appearance and home-screen widgets.

Premium subscriptions may be available through Google Play on monthly and annual plans. Pricing and availability can vary by country.

Flosi is a personal-finance management tool and does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
