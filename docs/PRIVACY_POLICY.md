# Flosi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Flosi is a personal finance application developed by Yam Studio and presented through Wana84.com.

## Data handled by Flosi
Flosi may store information that you enter into the app, including accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, goals, commitments, invoices, people/ledger records, preferences and backup data.

## Local financial data
Core financial records are designed to be stored locally on the user's device. Flosi does not claim ownership of this information.

## Authentication and cloud services
Where account authentication, subscription validation, crash diagnostics or other cloud-backed features are enabled, limited technical and account information may be processed by the relevant service provider solely to provide those functions.

## Payments and subscriptions
Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Flosi does not directly collect or store the user's full payment-card details.

## Backups
If the user creates or restores an encrypted backup, the backup is handled according to the backup feature selected by the user. Users are responsible for protecting exported backup files and any passwords or credentials used with them.

## Security
Flosi includes security controls intended to reduce unauthorized access, including optional biometric protection and secure-screen controls. No software can guarantee absolute security.

## Data retention and deletion
Users can remove financial records from the application and may uninstall Flosi to remove locally stored application data, subject to Android and any enabled cloud-service retention rules. Subscription history remains subject to Google Play policies.

## Children
Flosi is a general personal-finance tool and is not specifically designed for children.

## Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated as Flosi features, providers or legal requirements change. The revision date will be updated when material changes are made.
